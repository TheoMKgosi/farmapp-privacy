Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Farm Management App (the "App") collects, uses, stores, and shares your personal information, and explains how to contact us about your data.

1. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of data, and only the minimum needed to operate the App:

  • Account information: your name and email address, and a password if you register with email. Your password is protected with industry-standard hashing and is never stored in plain text.
  • Social sign-in: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive only the profile information you authorize Google to share (such as your name and email).
  • Farm data and content: records, notes, and photos you create in the App, including images captured with the camera. This may include information about your farm, crops, and livestock.
  • Device and diagnostics: device model, operating system, app version, and error logs used to keep the App stable and fix crashes.
  • Payment information: handled by a secure third-party payment processor or the app store. We do not store your full card number or card security details.

2. Why we collect it

Your data is used to:

  • Create and manage your account and secure sign-in.
  • Store and sync your farm data across your devices.
  • Deliver push notifications you have opted into.
  • Process purchases and manage subscriptions.
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix errors to improve reliability.

3. How we share data

We rely on trusted third-party services to operate the App. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising. Your data is shared only with:

  • Clerk — account authentication and sign-in.
  • Supabase — cloud database and storage for your farm data.
  • Sentry — error and crash monitoring; may include limited device and diagnostic data.
  • Expo notifications — delivering push notifications.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google, and only the profile data you authorize.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy and is bound by the purposes described here.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal obligations (for example, transaction records).

You can delete your account directly in the App using the account deletion button. Deleting your account removes your account and associated farm data. Some records may be retained where legally required.

5. Data storage and transfer

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside of Botswana by our service providers. We take reasonable security measures to protect your information wherever it is processed.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including encryption of data in transit, hashed passwords, restricted access, and the security practices of our processing partners. No method of storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to request correction or deletion. To exercise these rights, or to ask us to delete your data manually, contact us using the details below. We respond within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Policy updates

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you in the App or by email and update the effective date at the top of this page.
