Delete Your Account and Data

This page explains how you can permanently delete your Farm Management App account and the data associated with it. The App is developed by Theo Kgosiemang.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, directly in the App, using either of these options:

Option 1 — In-app (recommended, fastest):

  1. Open the Farm Management App.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm when prompted.

Option 2 — By request (email):

  1. Send an email to theo.kgosiemang12@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. In the message, include the email address you used to sign up for the App.
  4. You may be asked to verify your identity to confirm you are the account owner.

We process requests within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the following data associated with your account from our active systems:

  • Your account profile (name, email, and auth details).
  • Your farm records, notes, and saved data.
  • Photos and media you created in the App.
  • Your push-notification registration (you will no longer receive notifications).

What is kept, and why

A small amount of data may be retained after deletion where required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Transaction records: If you made purchases with us, we may retain limited financial records (not including your card details) for legal and accounting obligations. Card payment data is held by our payment processor, not by us.
  • Derived or non-identifiable data: Aggregated, anonymized statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be kept for product improvement.

Retained transaction data is kept only for the period required by applicable law (subject to Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2011 and related obligations), and in no case longer than necessary.

If you signed in with Google

Deleting your account in the App removes your data from the Farm Management App. It does not delete your Google account. To remove the App's access to your Google account, review your Google account permissions and revoke access if you wish.

Any questions?

Contact us at theo.kgosiemang12@gmail.com and we will help you delete your account and data.
